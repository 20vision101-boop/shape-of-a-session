THE IDEA

Same method, four systems

Each page takes one measurable thing, shows the curve rather than the average, explains the mechanism underneath it, and then gets practical: what moves it, how to do those things properly, and a place to put your own numbers.

Nothing you enter leaves your browser. Every panel on this site stores to localStorage on your own device — there's no backend, no account, and nothing to sign up for.

HOW THEY CONNECT

Four systems, one set of levers

The pages keep arriving at the same handful of things, which is the most useful result on the site. Sleep appears on all four. Body composition appears on three. Training bends the glucose curve acutely and the other two over months. Cortisol raises blood glucose directly and suppresses the gonadal axis when it stays up.

None of it is a protocol, and none of it replaces a doctor — the point is to make the mechanisms legible enough that you can ask better questions about your own numbers.
